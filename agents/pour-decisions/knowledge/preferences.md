# What makes a tasting worth it

Denis is WSET 3 certified and curious about every style, region, and producer. Masha comes along and loves wine and food at every level (see `shared/knowledge/household.md`). Write for a nerdy reader: grape, place, vintage, élevage, and producer detail are welcome.

## Scoring (Pour score, 1–10)
Start at 5 and adjust.

**The big one: a meaningful structure (+2 to +4).** A tasting that teaches something by comparison:
- One grape, different terroirs (e.g. Sangiovese from three Chianti villages, Cabernet across appellations)
- Verticals (one wine, several vintages)
- Different aging or élevage (oak vs. amphora vs. steel, NV vs. vintage, library vs. current release)
- One producer's single vineyards / crus / contrade side by side
- Blind tastings and structured seminars

**Also adds:**
- Winemaker, owner, or Master Sommelier hosting (+1)
- Rare, allocated, cult, library, or first-US-release bottles (+1 to +2)
- Good value: retail cost of the pours is well above the ticket price (+1)
- A region, grape, or style we'd rarely get to taste (+1)

**Takes away:**
- Generic themes with no through-line ("Napa favorites", "Wine After Dark", "Oktoberfest") (−1 to −2)
- Mainly a party, food night, or celebrity appearance (−2)
- Lineup not published (don't penalize much; say it's unknown and judge on producer and format)
- More than 15 miles from 90025 (−1), except the Wally's locations listed in `sources.md`

**Filtered out entirely (not scored):**
- Spirits, beer, sake-only events — until the whiskey expansion is turned on (see below)
- Members-only events we can't attend (e.g. K&L Wine Club pickup)
- Anything outside the allowed locations (Wally's Las Vegas, K&L Hollywood/SF/Redwood City)
- Past events

WSET and other multi-week courses aren't tastings; mention them in one line under "Classes" at most.

## Tiers
- **8–10 → Book now.** Full write-up.
- **6–7 → Worth a look.** Full write-up, shorter.
- **≤5 → Also on.** One line each.

## Timing
- Look ahead **8 weeks**. Big tent events and festivals: include up to 3 months out.
- Weeknight and weekend events are both fine.

## Categories
- **wine:** on
- **whiskey / spirits:** off for now. When turned on: same rules, with verticals, cask finishes, and single-cask/distillery comparisons as the "meaningful structure".
