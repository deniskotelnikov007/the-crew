## Dead ends
Checked 2026-09-26. Don't suggest these again unless something changes.
- **Esters Wine Shop, Santa Monica:** closed.
- **AJA Vineyards, Santa Monica:** social wine bar (mixers, spa nights), no structured tastings.
- **Helen's Wines:** private or virtual classes only.
- **Total Wine, Culver City:** mass-market classes; site blocks bots.
- **Wine Expo (Santa Monica), Silverlake Wine, Vinovore:** casual weekly pours, nothing themed on the calendar.
- **Eventbrite, LocalWineEvents.com:** mostly noise (bourbon, cooking classes, themed dinners). Not worth scanning broadly.
- **Tre Bicchieri (Gambero Rosso) LA:** trade-only.
