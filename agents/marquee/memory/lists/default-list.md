### The Intouchables (2011)
Olivier Nakache & Éric Toledano · 112 min · French comedy-drama · Safe bet
Rotten Tomatoes: 75% critics · 93% audience
Awards: César Award, Best Actor (Omar Sy) · Golden Globe nominee, Best Foreign Language Film
Lists: IMDb Top 250

Synopsis: A wealthy Parisian aristocrat, paralyzed from the neck down, interviews a line of earnest caregivers and hires the one applicant who plainly doesn't want the job: a brash young man from the suburban projects who only came for a signature. Loosely based on a true story, it's an odd-couple friendship told with far more laughter than pity, and Omar Sy's energy carries every scene.

For you: Two opposite lives rubbing off on each other, funny and tender at once, the way *Good Bye Lenin!* (8/8) was. French with subtitles, well within both your limits.
Heads-up: Disability is the central theme; some cannabis use.
